The right online filing service depends on what you need done and how much you want to do yourself. Here are the questions to ask, followed by a neutral look at some well-known services.

## What kinds of online filing services exist?

- **Do-it-yourself software:** you enter the data and file; the software guides you
- **Assisted services:** a qualified professional prepares and files for you
- **Marketplaces:** a platform connects you with independent professionals
- **A local professional:** you deal directly with one person or firm

None of these is better for everyone. A salaried employee with one Form 16 may be happy with software, while a company with annual filings, GST and TDS usually wants someone accountable for the whole calendar.

## Which eight questions should I ask?

1. **Who does the work?** Ask whether a qualified professional prepares and reviews it, and whether that person is named.
2. **What exactly is included?** Check whether the fee covers the filing only, or also notices, corrections and follow-up.
3. **What is the price and what changes it?** Look for a clear quote and for what could add to it, such as government fees or extra documents.
4. **How do I share documents?** Prefer a secure upload over email or messaging apps.
5. **Can I see progress?** You should be able to see the status of your request without asking for it.
6. **How do I talk to the person doing the work?** A written thread with your documents attached beats scattered calls.
7. **What happens if something goes wrong?** Ask how mistakes, rejections and notices are handled.
8. **How is my data handled?** Ask about virus-scanned uploads, access control and who can see your documents.

## Which one should I pick?

Match the service to the job. For a one-off return, a self-service tool may be enough. For registrations and recurring filings, ask the eight questions above and compare the answers. Whichever you choose, keep your own copies of what you submit and the acknowledgements you receive.
